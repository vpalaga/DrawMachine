GPIO number summary
-------------------
GP2  Z end switch
GP3  Z DIR
GP4  Z STEP
GP5  Z EN
GP6  Y DIR
GP7  Y end switch
GP8  Y STEP
GP9  Y EN
GP10 X EN
GP11 X end switch
GP12 X DIR
GP13 X STEP
GP14 LED console mode
GP15 LED instruction
GP17 Button B2
GP18 Button B1
GP19 Manual jog Y-
GP20 Manual jog Y+
GP21 Manual jog X-
GP22 Manual jog X+

Unused by the firmware: GP0, GP1, GP16, GP26, GP27, GP28.
GP0, GP1 are ready for later uart usage

Notes
-----
- Switches and buttons are wired between the GPIO and GND and use the internal
  pull-up, so a closed switch reads as pressed.
- Stepper EN pins are active low (LOW = driver enabled).
- The manual jog buttons and B1/B2 are only used by manual_instruction(),
  which is currently commented out in main().
